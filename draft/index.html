A doctor.

Dr. Michelle Lu-Ferguson is a gynaecologist in Shanghai. She holds a medical degree and completed her residency training at Rutgers' Robert Wood Johnson Medical School.

Her experience spans clinical practice, medical education, and women's health leadership. She consults in English and Mandarin at Parkway MediCentre Xintiandi.
