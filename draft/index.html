01 / Meet Dr. Ferguson

A doctor.
A listener.
Your partner in care.

Good care begins with being heard.

Dr. Michelle Lu-Ferguson is an American board certified OB/GYN specialist and a Fellow of the American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists (FACOG).

Her experience spans clinical practice, medical education, and women's health leadership. She consults in English and Mandarin at Am-Sino Ding Xiang Clinic and Parkway MediCentre Xintiandi in Shanghai.

Hormone & Menopause Health

  • Hormone replacement therapy (HRT)

  • Hormone-related changes in mood, sleep and weight
02

Gynecologic Care

  • Cervical screening (HPV / TCT / colposcopy)

03 / Her work

Caring for women. Sharing what she knows.

Dr. Ferguson's work connects patient care with medical education and collaboration across specialties. Her experience spans clinical practice in the United States and women's health leadership in Shanghai.

Working together

Ferguson Plus

Part of a multidisciplinary health team in Shanghai.
More about her professional journey

Teaching, research & safer care

After her obstetrics and gynaecology residency at Robert Wood Johnson, Dr. Ferguson remained on the faculty, combining clinical care with teaching residents and medical students and conducting clinical research. Since returning to China in 2005, she has held leadership roles focused on care quality and patient safety in Shanghai.

Sharing knowledge beyond the clinic

Her work also includes health education for the wider community. In a 2026 Am-Sino medical career programme, she introduced Shanghai Community International School students to women's health from adolescence through menopause.
