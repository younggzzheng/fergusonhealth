02 / Meet Dr. Ferguson

Your partner in care.

Dr. Michelle Lu-Ferguson, MD, FACOG

Founder & President, Ferguson Women’s Health

Dr. Michelle Lu-Ferguson is an American board-certified OB/GYN specialist and a Fellow of the American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists (FACOG). With decades of clinical experience across the United States and China, she is dedicated to advancing evidence-based, cross-cultural women’s healthcare.

Clinical focus & medical training

Clinical focus

Her current practice spans the full spectrum of women’s health, with a particular focus on endocrine and hormonal care, menopause management, PMOS (Polyendocrine Metabolic Ovarian Syndrome), gynecologic diagnosis and treatment, fertility and preconception counseling, contraception and family planning, sexual and vulvovaginal health, adolescent gynecology, minimally invasive gynecologic procedures, pelvic floor health, and long-term preventive care. She also provides consultation for complex and high-risk prenatal situations, offering clear guidance and individualized support.

Medical training & education

Dr. Lu-Ferguson received her Doctor of Medicine degree from the Medical University of Ohio.

She completed her residency in obstetrics and gynecology at Rutgers Robert Wood Johnson Medical School, a major academic medical center in the United States. After residency, she joined the faculty, contributing to clinical care, medical education, and clinical research—experience that continues to shape her rigorous, evidence-based clinical style.

Earlier in her academic training, she completed pre-medical studies at Peking University and continued at Peking Union Medical College, one of the top medical colleges in China. She later pursued PhD-level research training at New York University, further strengthening her foundation in medical science and clinical research.

Care approach

Known for her rigorous clinical standards, international training, and thoughtful communication style, she is highly regarded among both local and expatriate communities.

Experience that listens. Care that feels personal.
