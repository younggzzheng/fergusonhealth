Different chapters bring different questions. Space to understand yours.

Hormone & Menopause Health
  • Perimenopause and menopause care
  • Hormone replacement therapy (HRT)
  • Polyendocrine Metabolic Ovarian Syndrome (PMOS, formerly PCOS) and endocrine assessment
  • Hormone-related changes in mood, sleep and weight

Gynecologic Care
  • Cervical screening (HPV / TCT / colposcopy)
  • Menstrual disorders and endometrial disease
  • Uterine fibroids, ovarian cysts and endometriosis
  • Vaginitis and vulvar skin conditions

Reproductive & Fertility Health
  • Fertility assessment (AMH and ovarian reserve)
  • Preconception planning and counseling
  • Early pregnancy care (up to 12 weeks)
  • High risk pregnancy consultation
  • Fertility preservation

Contraception & Family Planning
  • Intrauterine devices (IUDs)
  • Contraceptive implants
  • Individualized contraceptive medication choices

Sexual & Vulvovaginal Health
  • Sexual pain (dyspareunia and vaginismus)
  • Vulvar skin conditions (including lichen sclerosus)
  • Genitourinary syndrome of menopause (GSM)

Adolescent Health
  • Menarche and puberty counseling
  • Adolescent menstrual concerns
  • Sex education and contraceptive guidance

Minimally Invasive Surgery
  • Hysteroscopy
  • Laparoscopy
  • Minimally invasive management of fibroids and cysts

Pelvic Floor Health
  • Pelvic floor function assessment
  • Mild urinary incontinence
  • Postpartum pelvic floor rehabilitation

Women’s Preventive Health
  • Bone density and bone health
  • Cardiovascular and menopause-related risk assessment
  • Chronic conditions and hormone management
  • Weight and lifestyle medicine

02 / Meet Dr. Ferguson

Dr. Michelle Lu-Ferguson, MD, FACOG

Founder & President, Ferguson Women’s Health

Dr. Michelle Lu-Ferguson is an American board-certified OB/GYN specialist and a Fellow of the American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists (FACOG). With decades of clinical experience across the United States and China, she is dedicated to advancing evidence-based, cross-cultural women’s healthcare.

Her current practice centers on women’s endocrine and hormonal health, including menopause management, PMOS (Polyendocrine Metabolic Ovarian Syndrome), menstrual disorders, abnormal uterine bleeding, fibroids, ovarian cysts, contraception, sexual health, and cancer screening. She also provides consultation for complex and high-risk prenatal situations, offering clear guidance and individualized care.

She consults in English and Mandarin at Am-Sino Ding Xiang Clinic and Parkway MediCentre Xintiandi in Shanghai.

Medical training, surgery & approach

Medical training & education

Dr. Lu-Ferguson received her medical training at Peking University, Peking Union Medical College, New York University, and the Ohio University College of Medicine. She completed her residency at Rutgers Robert Wood Johnson Medical School and later joined the faculty, contributing to clinical care, medical education, and research.

Surgical experience & care approach

Dr. Lu-Ferguson has extensive experience in minimally invasive gynecologic surgery, including hysteroscopy and laparoscopy.

Known for her rigorous clinical standards, international training, and thoughtful communication style, she is highly regarded among both local and expatriate communities.

At Ferguson Health, we make space for your questions, explain your options clearly, and support you through each stage of life.

More about her professional journey

Teaching, research & safer care

After her obstetrics and gynaecology residency at Robert Wood Johnson, Dr. Ferguson remained on the faculty, combining clinical care with teaching residents and medical students and conducting clinical research. Since returning to China in 2005, she has held leadership roles focused on care quality and patient safety in Shanghai.

Sharing knowledge beyond the clinic

Her work also includes health education for the wider community. In a 2026 Am-Sino medical career programme, she introduced Shanghai Community International School students to women's health from adolescence through menopause.
